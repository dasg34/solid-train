# Feasibility

- Phase: `P1`
- TV UX fit: Medium to high
- Live data difficulty: Medium
- Recommended surface: `split comparison`
- Primary payload: `product comparison`, `top picks`, `review summary`, `single next action`
- Core dependencies: `product catalog`, price feed, review corpus, bias controls
- Demo scope: Compare 2-3 products, summarize trade-offs, and highlight one recommended next step.
- Trust boundary: Disclose ranking logic, avoid affiliate-style pressure, and keep claims tied to sourceable facts.
- Live blockers: Price freshness, review quality, and commercial-bias perception on a large shared screen.
- Korea-first notes: Comparison-heavy shopping is common, but the TV surface should stay restrained and decision-focused.
