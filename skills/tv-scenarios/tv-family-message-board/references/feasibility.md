# Feasibility

- Phase: `P1`
- TV UX fit: High
- Live data difficulty: Medium
- Recommended surface: `modular dashboard` or `center card`
- Primary payload: `shared reminders`, `school notices`, `birthdays`, `household tasks`
- Core dependencies: `shared family data source`, moderation rules, household role model
- Demo scope: Show today reminders, one school or family notice, birthdays, and a simple chores strip.
- Trust boundary: Treat the TV as a public household surface and default to low-sensitivity summaries over detailed personal notes.
- Live blockers: Privacy boundaries across family members and content moderation for shared notes.
- Korea-first notes: School notices, cram-school timing, and family reminders are strong local use cases for a living-room screen.
