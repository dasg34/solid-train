# Feasibility

- Phase: `P0`
- TV UX fit: High
- Live data difficulty: Medium
- Recommended surface: `fullscreen scene` or `side panel`
- Primary payload: `lead story`, `headline hierarchy`, `category grouping`, `short summaries`
- Core dependencies: `news feed`, category mapping, summary cleanup, thumbnail fallback
- Demo scope: Show one lead story, 4-6 secondary headlines, and compact category rails.
- Trust boundary: Preserve source attribution, sanitize external text, and avoid overconfident LLM rewrites for sensitive stories.
- Live blockers: Publisher licensing, moderation policy, and summary quality control.
- Korea-first notes: Prefer Korean publishers, KST timestamps, and topic ordering that matches local expectations.
