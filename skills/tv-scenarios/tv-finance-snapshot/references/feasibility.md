# Feasibility

- Phase: `P2`
- TV UX fit: Medium
- Live data difficulty: Medium to high
- Recommended surface: `restrained dashboard`
- Primary payload: `watchlist movement`, `exchange rates`, `budget reminders`, `market events`
- Core dependencies: `market data API`, exchange-rate feed, watchlist state, market-hours logic
- Demo scope: Show a compact watchlist, KRW exchange rates, and one market context card.
- Trust boundary: Avoid investment-advice tone, label delayed data, and keep summaries factual and source-bounded.
- Live blockers: Delayed pricing rules, financial compliance messaging, and data licensing.
- Korea-first notes: Support KRW-first formatting and domestic market context before broader global coverage.
